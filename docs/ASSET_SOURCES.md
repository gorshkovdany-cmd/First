# Verified, candidate asset sources (October 2026)

Use these as primary *verified CC0* packs; inspect the per-pack licence and document it before bundling originals or derivatives:
- Kenney Car Kit (45 models): https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit — CC0, supports OBJ/glTF/FBX.
- Kenney City Kit Commercial (50 models): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial — CC0.
- Kenney City Kit Industrial (40 models): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial — CC0.
- Kenney City Kit Suburban (40 models): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban — CC0.
- Kenney City Kit Roads (90 models incl. traffic lights): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads — CC0.
- Quaternius Cars Pack: https://quaternius.com/packs/cars.html — pack page currently says CC0 but current brand-wide terms may vary; confirm per asset prior to re-distribution.

Avoid blindly importing OBJ/glTF into PSP runtime. Convert offline to compact indexed meshes, UV atlas, 16-bit quantized positions; generate lower LOD and collision proxy. Include a manifest recording asset source URL, licence, poly count and texture footprint. No copyrighted GTA VI map or assets.
